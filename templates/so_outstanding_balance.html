SO Outstanding Balance

Open PP / partials that still have remaining S/O qty. Lines that are fully shipped, voided, or already closed in ERP are excluded. Open S/O qty with no PP yet (including the remainder after a partial PP) appears as a second line for that process sheet with status No PP assigned. Searching the PS number finds both the pushed qty and the leftover. Open qty = leftover of that PP partial after shipped qty (not the full S/O remaining on every partial). SO value = unit x SO qty x exch (shown once per S/O line on export). Outstanding = unit x that leftover x exch, so summing the column does not double-count split partials. Week from Coway EDD, else PO due. Export all includes every currently open line, not just the filtered view.
